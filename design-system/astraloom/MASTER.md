# Astraloom design system

## Direction

An imaginary observatory: Swiss editorial structure fused with a dark orbital instrument. The interface should feel authored, precise, and calm rather than like a generic crypto dashboard.

## Palette

- Day background `#f4f1ea`, surface `#fffdf8`, ink `#171b2c`, muted `#5e6475`, border `#d6d3cc`.
- Night background `#0d1020`, surface `#14182c`, ink `#f2f1fb`, muted `#a4a8c1`, border `#303653`.
- Violet action `#635bdb` / `#a89aff` in night mode.
- Signal gold `#d8b56d` for status and disclosed proof markers.

## Typography

Fraunces is reserved for expressive headlines. Manrope handles navigation, forms, metadata, and body copy. Keep body text at 14–16px with generous line-height.

## Layout and interaction

Use spacious asymmetric editorial layouts, thin borders, rounded 10–28px surfaces, and a measured grid motif only in the hero. Controls are at least 44px tall, every icon button has an accessible name, focus rings remain visible, and reduced motion disables non-essential animation. Day/night is a persistent user preference.

## Anti-patterns

No emoji as icons, no invented user counts or social proof, no fake deployment addresses, no unreadable gray-on-gray copy, no hover-only actions, no claims of total anonymity, and no copied botanical/previous-project language.
